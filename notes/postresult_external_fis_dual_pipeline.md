# Two analyses of the same FIS plate share track-label ambiguity

27 September 2026, 7:12 PM IST. Protocol `6d65127` was pushed before reading Fiji/ImageJ records beyond their header; CellProfiler issues were known. The source https://github.com/hmbotelho/FIS_analysis supplies two processing outputs for the *same* 448-image, 64-well one-plate demo, not two independent experiments. Combined CellProfiler CSV: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hmbotelho/FIS_analysis/master/demo_dataset/05-images_analysis/demoplate_01--cellprofiler--analysis/FIS_rawdata.csv , SHA256 `1cb65d37fe78ad232c9829634c4ed7be8afc52e9e0308a519673eb3a9d91f807`; combined Fiji/ImageJ CSV: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hmbotelho/FIS_analysis/master/demo_dataset/05-images_analysis/demoplate_01--ij--analysis/FIS_rawdata.csv , SHA256 `072554a0bf397adcaa53b83670626d8ed8e4e01d6617911534d0c5dc5bf10194`. Both have 64 wells x seven frames, identical four arm labels and eight positive forskolin levels; neither has zero-forskolin or inhibitor controls in these tables.

CellProfiler contains 31,900 object rows, 1,080 without labels, 4,265 within-well nonmissing track labels, and 929 well x time x label keys with multiple rows (965 excess rows, up to three). Fiji/ImageJ contains 29,291 object rows, zero missing labels, 4,327 within-well labels, 4,080 labels occurring both at t0 and final t6, and 66 duplicated well x time x label keys (66 excess rows, up to two). Fiji reduces but does not eliminate label collisions; absence of missing labels is not proof of correctness. These are processed outputs from different algorithms on the same plate, without independent object-level hand-tracked ground truth; do not merge them into a biological replication or treat nominal labels as unique individual trajectories. Exact counts, duplicate examples and hashes are in `results/postresult_external_fis_dual_pipeline.json`, rerun by `scripts/audit_postresult_external_fis_dual_pipeline.py` against downloaded source tables.

This reinforces the earlier limitation: prospective resegmentation/retracking with identity checks would be needed before testing baseline-size dependent per-object swelling in this distinct source. It cannot repair original VO3 failed sign/specificity gates or withdrawn benchmark-win assertion.
