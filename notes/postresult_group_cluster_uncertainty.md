# Candidate-scene cluster uncertainty for the 14-image exploratory eval

27 September 2026, after the 14-image AP50 values were observed. This is a post-result descriptive sensitivity, not a new holdout or an amendment to any original gate. The 0.95 image-only graph from `results/scene_graph_forensics.json` yields 12 candidate components on the reassigned eval set: 10 singleton components and two two-image components. The group is a proxy for scene similarity, not a known independent donor, plate, well or imaging session.

For this already outcome-aware sensitivity, the calculation was specified immediately before execution (not before the AP50 outcomes were seen): (1) per-group mean of author AP50 then equal average over 12 groups; (2) a seed-20260927 20,000-draw bootstrap of 12 groups with replacement, averaging group means per draw; and (3) the ordinary 14-image mean and existing image-bootstrap interval as comparators. Preserve both estimates even if group uncertainty is narrower. The group equal-weighted estimand differs from a 14-image file-weighted mean, so it cannot isolate a variance effect. Do not use this exploratory interval to reselect the model or to claim a benchmark win. Hidden group links can make either interval falsely narrow. Full source filenames and scores stay in the original eval JSON.
