# VO3-R3 judge-informed measurement-invariance and influence study

Date 2026-09-26. Status: EXPLORATORY follow-up locked before its own computations, not independent preregistration: the original size-LMM result, cutoffs and donor-level p-value have been seen. Motivated by ChatGPT judge round 04, but judge content is advice, not evidence or authorization. No change to VO3-R2 or sealed segmentation G1.

Research question: does the observed treatment-by-baseline-size interaction remain numerically stable under donor influence and unequal cell-count weighting, or could measurement and selective tracking explain it? The public `dis_merged_A0.csv` contains matched-particle `A0`/`A1`, donor, experiment, well, treatment and dose, but no mask shapes, tracking-confidence, missing-frame or excluded-track reasons. It CANNOT test segmentation erosion/dilation or survivor selection directly. A separate paired raw-tracks/images and manual-mask audit is necessary for those controls.

Locked exploratory diagnostics for the 31 previously eligible blocks, fixed 722/1400 cutoffs: (1) per-donor median-block effect, equal-donor mean, leave-one-donor-out equal-donor means (min/max and donor identity), and donor-bootstrap 95% percentile interval with seed 20260926 and 20,000 draws; (2) block-level treatment-minus-DMSO, large-minus-small effect under ordinary cell means and a robust within-cell 20% trimmed mean, with the the original 31-block eligibility and a second equal-donor-then-median-block summary; (3) report distribution of four cell counts and inverse cell-count diagnostic so small cells are not mistaken for large cells; (4) A0-bin boundary sensitivity at fixed cutoffs ±10% (648/1260, 794/1540 rounded), recomputed from all originally eligible-source rows including middle-size objects, is descriptive only, not a new gate. Do not select a favorable threshold, p-value or donor subset. Every sensitivity output, including reversal, is recorded. No new discovery conclusion on reused accession.

Mechanistic falsification for future independent data: before outcomes, lock a new cohort and mask/track audit. Run identical donor-block interaction under raw/eroded/dilated/manual area measurements and inverse-probability weighting by retention probability estimated from initial size, condition, donor and imaging batch. If the interaction changes sign, substantially shrinks under plausible segmentation perturbations, or disappears with retention weighting, reject biological-invariance interpretation. Conversely, surviving does not prove causality without wet-lab controls. Preserve the original no-forskolin specificity failure.

Segmentation follow-up: separate fresh, fully labeled acquisition-shift cohort; freeze v2 weights, normalization, postprocessing and images before seeing labels, compare AP50, AP75, per-object size bias and donor/camera strata. Do not recycle the sealed 12-image gate. The published 0.76 is rounded, and the observed 0.001108 crossing is too small to assert general superiority.
