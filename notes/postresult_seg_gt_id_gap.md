# Author-vs-unique-count AP50 discrepancy in the 14-image exploratory eval

27 September 2026, after both scorer outputs were known. This is a metric-accounting audit, not a new model test. The pinned OrgaSegment v1.0.1 scorer uses the maximum ground-truth label as the count of objects; our alternate implementation counts positive labels actually present. When the ground-truth label numbers skip one ID, the two methods can disagree without different predicted masks or matching geometry. The exploratory eval stores both scores and the ground-truth gap count for every image. Verify that the discrepancy is limited to exactly the images with a label-ID gap, and report per-image TP/FP/FN. Neither version is a published-model baseline, and author AP50 is the primary reported number in this exploratory arm. Do not let a metric accounting difference become a biological conclusion or a gate repair.
