The released source has x/y centers at both timepoints for positive-area paired objects; a scout found all 29,648 positive-area pairs have <=50 pixel center displacement, strongly suggesting a track-matching support boundary but not proving the source algorithm's rule. The 31 original blocks, size cutoffs and outcome are known, so this is explicitly post-result exploratory and NOT a new biological gate. The source author algorithm should not be asserted without its code. Missing endpoint rows have no x/y and are outside this analysis; they cannot be assigned displacement zero.

Freeze fixed displacement bins [0,10), [10,25), [25,40), [40,50] pixels, with >50 separately if present. On all positive A0/A1 source pairs in original 31 donor x experiment x positive dose blocks, fixed small A0<722 versus large A0>=1400 and two original arms, compute every arm/size bin count, median log(A1/A0), and fraction displacement >=40. This is an influence check, not a newly chosen exclusion criterion. Negative controls: (i) DMSO-only within-cell Spearman rank correlation between displacement and log(A1/A0), stratified by small/large and with per-well directional medians to avoid treating objects as independent donors; (ii) report treatment-versus-DMSO displacement differences per size from all positive pairs. Report exact source hash, maximum observed displacement, all four-cell counts, per-donor effects, and any sign-count shift. A residual observed contrast under <40 does not correct unknown missing endpoints or tracking errors within the radius; a large change would narrow the paired-track interpretation. No endpoint-imputation, no new p-value, no revised sign/specificity gate.
