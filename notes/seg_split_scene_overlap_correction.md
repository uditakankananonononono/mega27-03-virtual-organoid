# Post-result scene-overlap correction (2026-09-26)

The v2 model was trained on the 184 images labeled `train` and its checkpoint/postprocessing was selected from files labeled `val`; the 12 files labeled `eval` were scored only after selection. These procedural facts are retained. The stronger assumption that the acquisition scenes themselves were independent was not checked at that time.

After the saved AP50 0.761108 crossing, `scripts/audit_seg_split_integrity.py` scanned all 231 images. Exact SHA256 image and mask overlaps across splits were zero. Five distinct-file cross-split pairs exceeded standardized-thumbnail cosine 0.95. At least the top train/eval pair and two train/val pairs depict the same organoids in the same layout on direct image inspection; a fourth val/eval pair may be a shifted field, and a fifth requires review. This is a scene-overlap flag, not a proof that every other pair is independent. The complete filenames, hashes, cosine scores and deterministic manifest digest are in `results/seg_split_integrity_v2.json`. Original pixels reside in the public OrgaSegment Basic dataset (Zenodo 10278229).

Interpretation: the preregistered numeric criterion >0.76 was crossed on those evaluation files, but those files do not constitute an independently unseen acquisition sample. **Withdraw benchmark-beat and independent held-out claims.** Preserve the sealed numerical result and negative v1; do not remove suspect images to rescore post hoc, retune to the existing eval split, or alter the gate. Repair requires acquisition metadata or defensible scene group IDs, group-disjoint retraining/validation, and a genuinely untouched independent evaluation cohort with paired author-model predictions. The size-response biological finding is separate and retains its own limitations.
