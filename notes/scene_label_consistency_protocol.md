# Post-result repeated-field annotation consistency, 26 September 2026

Question: do visually repeated acquisition fields also share labeled organoid objects, explaining why a file-level split is not independent? This is a retrospective descriptive check after the old AP50 was scored. It is not a repair, replacement gate, or revised benchmark.

Use only the three cross-split candidate pairs that survived image-only threshold 0.99 in `results/scene_graph_forensics.json`; the grouping was fixed without reading masks or predictions. For each pair, load the original 512x512 instance masks. Compute pixelwise IoU for every labeled-object pair, match one-to-one with Hungarian assignment maximizing IoU, and report count >=0.5, count >=0.75, median matched IoU, and weighted foreground-mask Dice. Image alignment is not adjusted. Also compare two predefined descriptive controls: the image-only 0.95 flagged false-positive candidate (train/0003 vs val/0003) and a retrospectively selected cross-split 512x512 pair (train/20210727_11104 versus eval/20210727_26838) with distinct layouts, whose exact filenames are saved in the result. A truly shared field can change slightly between frames and annotation sessions, so unmatched objects do not prove distinct biological objects. The controls do not create a null distribution or inferential p-value. No model score, checkpoint, training or eval images are altered.
