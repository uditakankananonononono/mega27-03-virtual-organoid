# 2021 iPSC-derived intestinal CFTR FIS prior-art and supplement audit

Preregistered at commit cbd016a before downloading supplement. Article https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4409/10/12/3419 , supplementary archive https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/cells/cells-10-03419/article_deploy/cells-10-03419-s001.zip . The archive has one PDF (`cells-1444336-supplementary.pdf`, 1,436,856 bytes), not raw CellProfiler tracks or an object table. Full text's Data Availability Statement (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC8699884/fullTextXML) says study data are available from corresponding author upon request.

Methods 2.3 uses time-resolved tracking of *individual* human intestinal iPSC-derived organoids and measures size relative to the initial time point; it excludes incomplete tracks. Results 3.1 has a single named CF iPSC line CF1 and its F508del-CFTR rescue with VX-809/VX-770, alongside corrected/non-CF controls. This is direct prior art against broad claims that tracking an individual intestinal CF organoid in FIS is new. It is neither a multi-CF-donor paired-object treatment release nor a test of donor-matched drug-by-starting-size moderation. No raw-treatment outcome analysis and no accession gate count.
