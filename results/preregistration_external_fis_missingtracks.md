# Pre-registration: external demo untracked-object bounding and attrition mechanism audit

Existing paired-track dataset and sensitivity: `results/external_fis_demo.json` and `results/external_fis_robustness.json`; 130 baseline rows with missing tracking labels and a 16.7-point large-track attrition gap under drug were found. Before inspecting reanalysis, lock the 32 cached raw CellProfiler object CSVs and prior global area thresholds <=2042.626642 and >=6700.811789 μm².

M1: report for each condition and each size bin (small/large/middle) among ALL positive-area time-0 objects: counts with valid unique positive track ID, missing/duplicate label count and tracked-to-time-6 share. Compare large-object size distribution of lost vs retained tracks using median starting area per condition. Do not infer missing outcomes.

M2 sensitivity for each dose, calculate four-cell mean log(A60/A0) under two artificial extreme assignments for all baseline-positive objects without a usable endpoint: one sets log-swelling to the 5th percentile of observed matched outcome in the same condition/dose/size cell, the other to its 95th percentile; then construct the drug-minus-vehicle large-minus-small effect under the most adverse assignment (drug-large and control-small at 5th percentile; drug-small and control-large at 95th) and the reverse best case. Include missing-label baseline objects as unobserved endpoints. Require >=5 observed matched tracks in each cell (as earlier); report each dose and count of extreme-envelope positive signs. This is a bounding sensitivity, NOT imputation or a probability model; p-values on these artificial outcomes are not computed. If even worst-case median across doses remains positive, flag robust to this within-observed-quantile envelope. Otherwise say the observed effect is vulnerable to plausible missingness. The 5th/95th choice was fixed here, after discovering attrition, and is not a new independent experiment.
