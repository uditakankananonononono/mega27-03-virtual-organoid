# Pre-registration: independent multi-patient well-level intestinal FIS analysis

Source (metadata inspected, data not yet downloaded): Drevinek et al., *Journal of Cystic Fibrosis* 2021, DOI 10.1016/j.jcf.2021.07.006, data record https://zenodo.org/records/4771466 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.4771466). Its `area_data.csv` is described as well, time, area, anonymized patient, date/plate, technical replicate, condition (`TEZ/IVA`, `ELX/TEZ/IVA`, `FskOnly`) and forskolin concentration. Unlike the prior OrgaSegment and Botelho sources, this is a different patient cohort. The area is at the **well** level, not individual organoid area; unpublished raw images cannot be reconstructed from it. This cannot directly replicate small-organoid attenuation. This analysis tests treatment-ranking stability across patients and wells, not a novel clinical claim.

Freeze the following before downloading the zip. Keep only rows with finite positive area, nonnegative finite time and valid patient/date/plate, well, type and forskolin dose. Uniquely identify a well by patient + date + filename + well + condition + dose + replicate. Duplicate identical well/time measurements collapse to one; conflicting duplicates exclude that well/time and count them. For each well with time zero and at least one later common time, calculate normalized trapezoidal AUC of area(t)/area(0) over the intersection of recorded time points in its patient-date-plate-dose block across all three conditions, divided by the observation horizon. Exclude blocks with <3 common time points including zero or <2 wells per treatment arm; report exclusion reasons. Never extrapolate a time point. If `filename` is absent, use patient+date as plate and flag the change. FskOnly is a negative-control reference, not a vehicle for correcting object sizes.

Within a patient-date-plate-dose block calculate the **equal-well** mean normalized AUC for each condition. The primary block contrast is ELX/TEZ/IVA minus TEZ/IVA. The FskOnly-adjusted contrast is algebraically the same but report each arm minus FskOnly for context. Aggregate the median contrast across dose/plate blocks for each patient, then give patients equal weight. H1: at least 6 eligible patients, at least 75% of patients with positive contrast, and a one-sided exact binomial sign test p<0.05 among nonzero patient contrasts. Report all patients, blocks, well counts, effects, confidence/uncertainty, and exact p. If coverage fails, mark H1 uninterpretable. An H1 pass is independent patient-level support for the published triple-versus-double **in vitro** ranking, not a new discovery or patient-level treatment recommendation.

Pre-specified challenge tests: (1) raw area AUC ranking without baseline normalization and fraction of patient-block rankings that reverse relative to the primary, explicitly acknowledging starting area confounding; (2) leave-one-patient-out sign/effect and coverage; (3) median-of-wells rather than mean-of-wells; (4) per-dose effects without counting correlated doses as independent patients; (5) if sample permits, plate/time missingness by arm. Preserve any negative, reversal, dropout, or too-small result; do not optimize an effect threshold after viewing outcomes. Different genotype distributions, patient severity, assay and plate map prevent direct pooling with the prior single-donor demo or OrgaSegment. No diagnosis or treatment advice.
