# Pre-registration: independent FIS demo size effect (Botelho/Hagemeijer)

Distinct public intestinal-organoid FIS demonstration source: https://github.com/hmbotelho/FIS_image_analysis, `demo_dataset/05-images_analysis/demoplate_01--cellprofiler`, object CSVs with `TrackObjects_Label_4`, `Metadata_timeNum`, and `Math_area_micronsq`. Source README says a single plate, 64 wells, one class-II CFTR genotype, seven time points, DMSO/vehicle (`fsk`), VX-809 (`fsk_809`), VX-770 (`fsk_770`), combined (`fsk_770_809`), and matched forskolin doses. This is NOT the OrgaSegment accession; different laboratory assay and drugs. The two sample CSV headers were inspected to establish schema, but full set has not been fetched or analyzed. Commit before full download.

Fixed primary contrast: `fsk_770_809` vs `fsk`, at the SAME forskolin dose. Follow the README's one-plate map; treat each well as a separate experimental unit, pair object rows at `Metadata_timeNum=0000` and `0006` by `TrackObjects_Label_4` within well, keep only positive finite area and unique track/time rows (ambiguous duplicate labels excluded). Outcome log(A60/A0); starting size per well is the pooled median of its usable tracks, then create SMALL and LARGE using the pooled 25th and 75th percentiles of all tracked A0 across the selected vehicle/combination wells (quantiles calculated only after download, definition fixed here). In each of eight dose values represented in both conditions, compute [combo-large minus vehicle-large] - [combo-small minus vehicle-small], requiring >=5 tracks per cell and at least two wells per condition at that dose. H1: at least four eligible dose strata and positive median dose contrast with one-sided exact sign p<0.05. Report all dose/cell/well counts, excluded tracks, and sign p. If the dose/well floor fails, UNINTERPRETABLE, not zero. No imputation of missing tracks. Secondary exploratory per-object slope and Fiji output comparison, not part of H1. A positive result would be external assay-level support for size moderation, *not* independent donor replication or clinical/diagnostic confirmation; one donor and reused plate under multiple doses are not independent patients. A negative is preserved and lowers confidence in generalizing to the older VX-809/VX-770 combination.
