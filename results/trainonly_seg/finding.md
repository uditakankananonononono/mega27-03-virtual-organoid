# Train-only / val-only segmentation did not match the published benchmark

Protocol pre-registered and committed at `f0781d5`; implementation `scripts/train_seg_trainonly.py`. Dataset Zenodo 10278229: 184 train images, 35 disjoint val, 12 untouched eval. Sixty U-Net epochs fit on **train only** with fixed seeds and augmentation. Checkpoint at epoch 40 had the minimum validation cross-entropy, 0.075751 (all checkpoint values in `history.json`). Then a predeclared 108-combination postprocessing grid was scored on the first 20 validation images only. Its maximum val mean AP50, **0.81098**, chose (foreground .4, seed .5, boundary .3, minimum size 40 scaled to image area), frozen in commit `120f944` before evaluation. Validation AP is a selection metric, not an independent benchmark result.

The sole final evaluation on 12 images scored mean AP50 **0.738672 with the original released OrgaSegment/Cellpose matcher**, or 0.739328 with this repository's unique-instance-count matcher. Eleven images match to numerical rounding; the 12th source mask has missing GT label ID 17 and the authors' max-ID rule overcounts one instance. Per-image AP, TP, FP, FN, actual object counts and mask-label gaps are in `sealed_eval.json`. Published OrgaSegment Mask R-CNN mean is **0.76 +/- 0.12** on those 12 images (https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-05966-4); this clean pipeline is **below**, not a match or a win. Its improvement over the earlier model's 0.73236 is small and not proof of superiority. The released Mask R-CNN itself was not run here because its evaluation script requires unavailable TensorFlow 1.15/GPU; the published comparator has no paired per-image predictions in our result. Do not treat numerical closeness, validation performance, or the leakage correction as a novel model breakthrough. No patient-level or diagnostic inference.

Evidence and source: https://github.com/Living-Technologies/OrgaSegment/tree/v1.0.1 ; code `scripts/select_trainonly_postprocess.py`, `scripts/eval_trainonly_sealed.py`; selection `results/trainonly_seg/val_selection.json` and `results/trainonly_seg/sealed_eval.json`.
