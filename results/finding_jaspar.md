# JASPAR coverage of missing master TFs

Pre-registered at 0ebc540 before querying JASPAR CORE vertebrate matrices at https://jaspar.elixir.no/api/v1/matrix/ . Of the three fixed brain masters not covered by the earlier CollecTRI/decoupler network, NEUROD2 (MA0668.3) and TBR1 (MA0802.2) have exact-name motifs; NEUROD6 did not have an exact-name motif in the searched CORE vertebrate collection. Kidney PAX2 (MA0067.3) also has a motif. The coverage-only H1 passes (2/3 brain and PAX2). Full query URLs and motif URLs: `results/deficit_jaspar.json`.

This is a technical feasibility check for a future motif-based analysis. A motif in a database does not establish its expression, binding, activity, or deficiency in organoids. It does not reverse the previously failed CollecTRI positive control and is not a discovery or diagnosis.
