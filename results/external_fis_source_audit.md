# Independent single-organoid FIS source audit, 25 Sep 2026

Scope: find a distinct cohort with individual baseline area, individual endpoint area, donor/line, forskolin + modulator and matched vehicle, for an external test of small-organoid modulator attenuation. This audit only checks suitability, not an exhaustive review.

- Demchenko et al., PLOS Computational Biology, 27 Oct 2025: https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013589 . Single-organoid *respiratory lung* FIS, two lines (one healthy, one CF), baseline/24h after **forskolin 10 μM**, not a donor-matched modulator vs DMSO assay. Methods explicitly select baseline area >=1500 px and fold change >=0.8 for swelling analysis. That size truncation excludes the earlier small A0 <722 px hypothesis. Public OSF project https://osf.io/4savy/?view_only=2163a86b20a5468989c041536752b19e had one example 0h/24h image pair and table files under Project_Example, plus respiratory segmentation-training images. The paper describes 44 healthy and 228 CF organoids for FIS; this is useful dataset context but not evidence for the modulator-specific claim. OSF read-only API project and file listing: https://api.osf.io/v2/nodes/4savy/files/osfstorage/?view_only=2163a86b20a5468989c041536752b19e . No download or outcome analysis was performed; no accession added to the primary-used ledger.
- Matthews et al., OrganoID, PLOS Computational Biology 2022: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9645660/ and open examples https://github.com/jono-m/OrganoID . Its individual-organoid Figure 3 time series are pancreatic cancer organoids under **gemcitabine**, not CFTR activation or modulator rescue. The public `Publication/Figure3/OrganoidMeasurements.csv` has time, individual area, dosage, replicate, but this endpoint mismatch prevents a genuine replication of CF intestinal FIS.
- Anderson et al., Am J Physiol Lung Cell Mol Physiol 2021: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8321858/ . Nasal epithelial FIS with CFTR modulators but the published measure is **sum total area per well**; their analysis excludes masks <90 or >500 μm and reports means over roughly 40 organoids/well. No paired individual-organoid baseline/endpoint table was identified in this source. It is not direct validation of the candidate.

These three are leads, not proof that no other usable public cohort exists. Do not pool different assays or count a source as an accession used simply because its metadata or article was inspected. External replication remains open.
- Drevinek et al., *Journal of Cystic Fibrosis* 2021, https://zenodo.org/records/4771466 : a genuinely independent 20-patient intestinal FIS cohort with well-level area trajectories for two patient modulator arms, but no public individual-organoid tracks or images. Pre-registered and used for an independent **well-level ranking** analysis in `results/finding_drevinek_fis.md`; not eligible for direct testing of small-organoid attenuation. FskOnly wells are from a reference line according to the released plate map, not patient-matched controls.
- Lo Cicero et al., *Cells* 2025, https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4409/14/23/1914 : 38 rare-genotype patient samples and individual manually delineated organoids before/after ETI or ivacaftor in the methods; the data statement says original contributions are in article/supplements and further inquiries to corresponding author. Public object-level baseline/endpoint table or raw images have not been established through this bounded search; summary plots cannot substitute for paired areas.
- Hill et al., *Genes* 2020, https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4425/11/6/603 : FIS describes summed total surface area per well for three illustrative subjects, with forskolin/IBMX rather than matched modulator and vehicle, and its public Zenodo https://zenodo.org/records/3838185 is supplementary material (~253 MB), not evidenced to contain per-organoid treatment pairs. Not downloaded for this unsuitable primary comparison.
- `hmbotelho/FIS_analysis` https://github.com/hmbotelho/FIS_analysis republishes `demoplate_01` object results from `hmbotelho/FIS_image_analysis` and is not a distinct cohort. `hmbotelho/organoid_analyst` https://github.com/hmbotelho/organoid_analyst did not show a candidate object table in its inspected repository tree. No accession was added for inspecting these repository trees.
- Bierlaagh et al., *Journal of Cystic Fibrosis* 2024, DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcf.2024.04.014, available manuscript https://lirias.kuleuven.be/retrieve/d84c084e-3705-4f59-8c29-e816fb0557ce : repeatability/reproducibility study; its Section 2.5 says relevant data and reference PDIO lines are available **on request** from the corresponding author. Public paired object-level data have not been verified. No request to the authors was sent.
- Lefferts et al., *International Journal of Molecular Sciences* 2023, https://www.mdpi.com/1422-0067/24/19/14539 : multi-genotype ETI/DMSO intestinal organoid study; its Data Availability Statement says data are available on request from corresponding author. This does not establish an openly retrievable paired-object dataset. A citation to its supplementary material is not, by itself, proof the exact required measurements are in it.
- FDA-approved drug screening in patient-derived organoids study, accessible manuscript https://research.vu.nl/ws/files/353806334/FDA-approved_drug_screening_in_patient-derived_organoids_demonstrates_potential_of_drug_repurposing_for_rare_cystic_fibrosis_genotypes.pdf : the stated public supplemental files contain tested-drug list and primary-screen AUC summaries. Its Data availability section says all other data are available upon request. Public individual baseline/endpoint organoid tracks are not established from this source. No author outreach made.
- ErasmusMC-Bioinformatics/OrganoTrack https://github.com/ErasmusMC-Bioinformatics/OrganoTrack : inspected the public repository tree; its example `trackedMeasures.xlsx` is under `experiment-cisplatin-drug-screen`, a different drug and endpoint, not a CFTR/forskolin matched modulator assay. The `jvzonlab/organoidtracker` and `541435721/Orga-DATA` repository trees also revealed no obvious candidate raw CF FIS paired-object table in this bounded filename/tree check; absence from a tree search does not prove the data do not exist elsewhere.
- Borek-Dohalska et al., VTI/ETI public dataset https://zenodo.org/records/15754800 (concept link https://zenodo.org/records/14865872): 12 CF patient IDs, 33 plates, but only **well-aggregate** area trajectories; no public individual organoid pairs or raw plate images. The pre-registered VTI-minus-ETI patient-level ranking test is a negative, `results/finding_vti_eti.md`. Patient identifiers `58`, `68` overlap textually with the earlier Drevinek release after stripping `-CF`; anonymization across studies prevents assuming either the same or distinct person. This accession is one dataset, regardless of how many CSVs or plates.
- Bulcaen et al., *Cell Reports Medicine* 2024, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11148721/ ; DETECTOR tool https://github.com/RL-arch/detector and Dataverse https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/OZZRPG , replication-data deposit https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/GD17UG . Live Dataverse metadata lists 557 and 553 files respectively, largely the same tool/training examples with images for IDs 242-CF, 426-CF and 429-CF, not an independent second replication by virtue of two deposits. The tool's README describes **binary swelling classification** after prime editing, and the paper explicitly states DETECTOR does not measure continuous modulator response/individual surface area; its example GitHub images are only one experiment labeled 426-CF L227R. The paper's data statement offers additional reanalysis information on request. The metadata inspected did not establish patient-matched modulator-versus-vehicle individual area tracks in either deposit; these are not direct tests of our candidate, and no data were downloaded or counted.
- S737F/T465N rectal-organoid study, https://ojrd.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s13023-024-03334-3 : its own data availability section says datasets analyzed are available on reasonable request; the public article alone does not establish raw paired-object accessibility. No author outreach.
- Amatngalim et al., *Life Science Alliance* 2022, https://www.life-science-alliance.org/content/5/12/e202101320 : seven F508del/F508del nasal-airway donor FIS experiments with VX-809/VX-770, four with triple modulator, but the paper describes the assay readout as **total surface area of all organoids within a well**. The inspected Figures & Data page https://www.life-science-alliance.org/content/5/12/e202101320/tab-figures-data lists three representative videos and XLSX supplements for donor use, media compositions and primers, not a paired single-organoid area table. Its broad 'all data provided with manuscript' statement does not make summary figures object-level data. The three videos do not establish a multi-donor matched object dataset; none were downloaded.
- van Mourik et al., FIS/disease progression, medRxiv https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2021.02.01.21250609v1 : the public Data Availability statement says analyzed datasets are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request. The study's patient-level association is not a public matched-object modulator/vehicle source for the candidate.
- Li et al., *PLOS ONE* 2023, https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0295009 : CFTR W1282X prime editing in iPSC-derived airway/lung organoids; methods describe imaging approximately 25 organoids/well at seven-plus time points and automated organoid-size quantification. But the intervention is genome correction and Fsk stimulation, not patient-matched drug versus vehicle across multiple donors. Its supporting FIS figure https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0295009.s003 alone is not a paired individual area dataset. Not a direct replication source.
- General organoid pipeline study, https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0332418 : the visible methods focus on microbial supernatants and human intestinal organoid/monolayer growth, not a CFTR modulator FIS cohort; excluded by intervention/endpoint mismatch, not because its technique is low quality.
- Cordero et al., *European Respiratory Journal* 2021, DOI https://doi.org/10.1183/13993003.02426-2019 , accessible manuscript https://lirias.kuleuven.be/retrieve/a59eb16b-7073-42b7-96b3-8c8607a95f75 : multi-donor modulator FIS study, but Methods and Supplementary Methods explicitly compute **total organoid area in each well** from 15-60 organoids, normalized at time zero, then average duplicate well AUC and three experiment days. Its figures/tables cannot be reverse-engineered to paired individual organoid areas, regardless of number of donors.
- W1282X/G542X readthrough-drug PDIO study, accessible manuscript https://iris.unipa.it/retrieve/df664288-b189-4551-b02c-b229d8145414/PIIS2949774425015110.pdf : Figure captions show representative baseline/60-minute organoid images and treatment/DMSO controls, but Methods explicitly quantify **total organoid area per well** (15-60 organoids) each 10 minutes, not paired single objects. Its Data Availability section says full data are available from authors on request. This source cannot validate the size hypothesis without unavailable object-level measurements. No author request made.
- Lopes-Pacheco et al., *Cells* 2022, https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4409/11/1/136 : F508del/F508del intestinal organoid FIS after MCG1516A versus VX-661, CellProfiler-based area AUC; the public article reports aggregate results and does not identify an openly downloadable multi-donor paired single-object table in the inspected methods/source. CellProfiler use alone does not establish raw tracking availability. Suitability remains unverified, not a claimed absence of data.
- Eight-rare-variant PDIO study, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40291-025-00806-5 : ten-person intestinal organoid CFTR-modulator FIS cohort, CellProfiler/Organoids Analyst used to compute AUC, with patient/variant-level figure and table summaries. The inspected article and linked material did not establish a public per-organoid baseline/end area table, tracking IDs, or paired raw images by treatment. A named object-analysis tool is not equivalent to shared object data; direct replication remains unverified rather than impossible.
- `vergani-lab-ucl/cftr-scan` https://github.com/vergani-lab-ucl/cftr-scan : single-cell fluorescent CFTR trafficking/channel assay in HEK293 cells with DMSO examples, not three-dimensional organoid FIS. `fmi-basel/HT_OrganoidAnalysis` https://github.com/fmi-basel/HT_OrganoidAnalysis is a colonic stem-cell regeneration morphology pipeline, and `lu-lab/organoid-classification` https://github.com/lu-lab/organoid-classification provides classification features, not CFTR modulator/vehicle FIS. Repository tree/README checks only, with no data downloaded or counted.
- Dekkers et al., *Science Translational Medicine* 2016, accessible article https://dspace.library.uu.nl/server/api/core/bitstreams/31b3b3fa-f625-45f2-a362-1aadfac913d3/content : patient-derived rectal organoid modulator FIS and static lumen area. The FIS Methods quantify **total organoid area** from 20-80 organoids/well relative to t=0; static luminal area is manually marked as the sum in the same well. Thus, even this foundational multi-patient assay gives a well-total result rather than released paired individual baseline/endpoint size measurements. No accession counted for checking the article.
- Zenodo 16311731 https://zenodo.org/records/16311731 offers raw images, ROI and a morphometric CSV for 20 **brain** organoids under developmental patterning conditions at day 18, not CFTR FIS or matched modulator/vehicle. Its object-level format is superficially attractive but cannot support this claim; metadata-only check, no dataset downloaded.
- Bracq et al., SWIFT single-organoid workflow, https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-026-10768-x : tempting independent image-analysis pipeline. Its stated external organoid benchmark sources are **OrgaSegment** (our original accession) and OrganoID (pancreatic cancer/gemcitabine); it is not a third independent CFTR treatment cohort. The public `upvdg/SWIFT` repository https://github.com/upvdg/SWIFT contains image-analysis/tracking scripts, not an identified multi-donor drug/vehicle paired FIS table. The article's 'Supplementary Data 1' spreadsheet is source data for its paper, but this metadata/source audit alone does not establish another biologically independent paired FIS dataset. No data downloaded/count added.
- Foster et al., *J Pediatr Gastroenterol Nutr* 2026, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12780477/ : Incucyte size time series after 5 µM forskolin versus vehicle, but biological lines are three healthy controls and **one** UNC45A-deficient patient, not a multi-donor CFTR-modulator cohort. The reported figure summarizes means, not a linked, openly downloadable paired single-object table. Distinct biological question and insufficient replication for the candidate; no data downloaded or count added.
- EMC3 mouse intestinal epithelium study, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11901349/ : CFTR-dependent enteroid swelling after cAMP/calcium stimulation, but genotype comparison is in mouse epithelial EMC3 deletion, not patient-matched CFTR drug/vehicle. Not eligible for the proposed human patient-general size test; no dataset downloaded/count added.
- Zenodo https://zenodo.org/records/4478984 surfaced in a fresh search for modulator/vehicle raw data. - Zenodo https://zenodo.org/records/3742142, human nasal epithelial organoid supplement: metadata describes FIS video of one non-CF and one F508del/F508del line, among other ciliary/mucus media. - OrgLine Zenodo https://zenodo.org/records/16355179 is a curated detection/segmentation aggregate containing OrgaSegment and other unrelated tissue benchmarks, not a new patient treatment cohort. - ELX-02/G550X patient-organoid study, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10202470/ : strong *methodological* match. Methods state Fiji detects and tracks **individual** PDO cross-sectional area over time; 20-60 organoids/well, vehicle versus ELX-02, six G550X and five comparator G542X PDO cultures. Figure 1 gives pooled object AUC box plots and representative time course, not a retrievable per-object baseline/end track table. The linked public Figshare supplements https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.22335733 and https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.21912963 contain figure-only PDF/DOCX per live Figshare file listings; no object CSV or image-stack deposit is identified. Thus an independent patient cohort exists in the *publication*, but an open direct replication dataset was not established. No supplement or outcome data downloaded, no count added; do not infer no data could be requested.
- Bulcaen et al. 2024 companion protocol, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xpro.2024.103593 (accessible author PDF https://lirias.kuleuven.be/retrieve/e4ffce59-d72d-4b49-8034-bdf298427cb4): the prescribed DETECTOR output is a **binary swollen/not-swollen** phenotype for gene editing; GitHub `RL-arch/detector` and Harvard Dataverse `10.7910/DVN/OZZRPG` are the same source family already checked, not paired continuous modulator/vehicle single-object areas. Its data preparation specifies image training inputs, not another independent cohort. No data downloaded/count added.

- Lefferts/Beekman-related 28-person rare-variant ETI study, author manuscript https://dspace.library.uu.nl/server/api/core/bitstreams/cc1dd6b6-a977-4421-beb5-65a7b4864bc3/content : a cohort with matched DMSO/ETI FIS and a separate steady-state lumen area (SLA) phenotype. - Novel G85E/N1303K modulator combinations, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9016267/ : has rectal-organoid brightfield FIS and DMSO controls, but Methods explicitly quantify the **total organoid area in each image** with ImageJ masks, not paired single-object t=0/t=60 areas. - Osorio-Vasquez et al., *Cell Stem Cell* 2026, https://www.cell.com/cell-stem-cell/fulltext/S1934-5909(26)00228-6 : an independent chronic-pancreatitis pancreatic ductal organoid FIS study of nine patient lines, with CFTR potentiator and inhibitor perturbations. The article says up to ten organoids selected per condition, with representative bright-field images and 6-hour swelling summaries. In the inspected accessible article, no open patient-by-treatment paired single-object baseline/end tracks were identified; pancreatic CP is also a different tissue/condition from the intestinal CF size hypothesis. This is a plausible follow-up to request raw data, not an established open direct replication. No data downloaded or accession counted.
- Hof et al. epithelial morphogenesis raw-image deposit, https://zenodo.org/records/4419985 : light-sheet/brightfield pancreas and cholangiocarcinoma organoid development, up to seven days, including individual lumen oscillation. - Calucho et al., *Scientific Reports* 2021, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-94798-x : nasospheroids from seven WT and seven CF people, with single-sphere FSK **shrinking** and separate VX-770+VX-809 / VX-770+VX-661 modulation in CF samples. Related prior art specifically tested initial size: among 138 WT FSK responders, baseline cross-sectional area vs 60-minute fraction had Spearman r=-0.1216 (p=.1537) and baseline vs AUC r=-0.1632 (p=.0550); supplementary text also reports slope correlation r=.04124 (p=.6298). Thus size-response analysis in CFTR spheroids is not new in general, and this airway model found no baseline-size effect. It is a different tissue, polarity and shrinkage readout, and did not establish an open per-CF-patient object-level paired modulator/vehicle table. The linked supplementary DOCX https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41598-021-94798-x/MediaObjects/41598_2021_94798_MOESM1_ESM.docx (inspected) contains results, model tables and figures rather than a verified raw paired-object dataset. It was read as literature, not counted as a primary accession; no replication result computed.
- Kim et al., *SLAS Discovery* 2020, https://www.slas-discovery.org/article/S2472-5552(22)06605-9/fulltext : earlier tracked colorectal tumor organoids explicitly compared initial size with growth rate in two patient-derived lines, found no clear relation, and excluded small organoids (<50 cells) for more variable rates and possible GFP/Imaris surface-detection failure. This is prior art for size-dependent measurement reliability and post hoc size filtering, not CFTR FIS or a matched-modulator intestinal dataset. No data downloaded or accession counted.
