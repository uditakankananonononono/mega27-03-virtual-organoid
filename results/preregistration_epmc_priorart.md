Keep first 50 results per query, title/DOI/PMID/abstract and source URL, deduplicate by PMID/DOI. H1 is a **novelty-falsification condition**: any result whose title/abstract explicitly reports starting organoid size modifying CFTR-modulator-induced FIS in intestinal patient-derived organoids defeats an unqualified novelty claim. Title/abstract absence cannot establish novelty; it merely leaves it unresolved. Human assessment of candidates precedes any conclusion. No claim that a search count is a scientific discovery, and a related airway finding must be reported. This prior-art audit is independent of the biological GSEA/HPO work.
