# Direct KEGG REST pathway check

Registered at 32f57b9 before direct download. Source: https://rest.kegg.jp/list/pathway/hsa plus the nine `url` fields in `results/deficit_kegg.json`; API documentation: https://www.kegg.jp/kegg/rest/keggapi.html. The fixed pathway-title substrings selected nine pathways and 218 distinct Entrez IDs, each mapped unambiguously to a symbol using the previously downloaded NCBI human gene_info table. All five fixed 100-gene lists are analyzed. Direct KEGG REST is a distinct service interaction for the tool ledger, but earlier g:Profiler work included KEGG annotations, so it is not an independent biological source or novel result.

Liver seven of 100 top deficits (CYP2A6, CYP2D6, CYP8B1, DPYS, SLC27A5, SULT2A1, UPB1) overlap the selected gene-set union versus zero brain-cortex deficits. One-sided Fisher exact p=0.0070089. NCBI mapping covers 99 liver and 100 brain genes, above the registered 80-gene floor. Kidney is four, lung one, colon zero. This supports annotation consistency only. Genes in KEGG/WikiPathways/GOA share pathway literature and curators; contrast is exploratory after the deficiency was identified. It does not show metabolic impairment in organoids, diagnostic utility, causality, or novelty. The zero brain overlap also reflects the narrow preselected metabolism set, so no general liver-versus-brain quality claim follows.
