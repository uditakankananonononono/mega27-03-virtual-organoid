# No-forskolin specificity control fails

The test was frozen and pushed in `results/preregistration_zero_fsk_control.md` at commit 788d802 before computing effects. It uses the already-used OrgaSegment DIS source (https://zenodo.org/records/10610438), the same drug/control arms and post hoc size thresholds as the registered blocked analysis, and only donor x experiment pairs with at least three measured organoids in every arm x size cell at **both** 0 and 0.128 µM forskolin. It asks whether the size interaction becomes more positive when CFTR is stimulated than in the same experiment without forskolin. That is a necessary specificity stress test for the proposed interpretation, not a definitive CFTR mechanism test.

Of 50 donor-experiment combinations in the two selected arms/doses, 24 experiment pairs across 11 donors qualify; six of the 17 source donors have no paired eligible experiment. Only **3/11** donors have positive median paired deltas (effect at 0.128 minus effect at 0), exact one-sided sign p=**0.96728515625**. The frozen >=8-donor rule is met, but H1 **FAILS**. In several donors the no-forskolin size interaction is more positive than the forskolin interaction. Full paired plate effects, cell minima and donor medians are in `results/zero_fsk_control.json`; no favorable dose or donor subset was selected afterward.

This failure further weakens a CFTR-specific small-organoid attenuation claim. It does **not** show that CFTR modulation has no effect: drug pretreatment, baseline growth, maturation, surface-area tracking and selection can affect the zero-forskolin arm, and swelling's scale differs between doses. The thresholds were selected using the same source, and these 11 donors are not external replication. The prior registered positive-forskolin H1 remains 9/12, p=.072998, FAIL; no diagnosis or new discovery follows.
