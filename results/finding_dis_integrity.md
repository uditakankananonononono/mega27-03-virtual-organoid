# OrgaSegment supplied-table integrity check

Preregistered before inspection at commit 12b6575. Running `scripts/audit_dis_integrity.py` on the existing `data/raw/orgasegment/dis_merged_A0.csv` (Zenodo https://zenodo.org/records/10610438) gives 28,526 rows, 17 donor labels, no duplicate full rows, no duplicate `ID` values and no collision in the diagnostic `(donor, experiment, well, condition, dose, particle)` key. A0, A1 and swelling have no missing, nonfinite or nonpositive values. Recorded swelling equals A1/A0 to floating precision: maximum relative discrepancy 2.15e-15, zero above 1e-6. The five largest (still floating-precision) discrepancies are preserved by source ID in `results/dis_integrity.json`.

The dose distribution is unequal: the 0.320 and 0.800 µM arms include only two donor labels each, versus 17 donors at 0.128 µM for DMSO and triple treatment. This audit finds no arithmetic or identifier defect in the supplied rows, but says **nothing** about whether tracking, cell sampling, segmentation, or treatment assignment is unbiased. It does not rescue the matched-block negative and adds no new biological inference. No new accession was fetched or counted.
