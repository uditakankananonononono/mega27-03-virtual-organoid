# Pre-registration: HPO clinical phenotype annotation of liver deficits

Before downloading HPO gene-to-phenotype annotations, register source https://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/hp/hpoa/genes_to_phenotype.txt (current release URL from official HPO repository) and https://obophenotype.github.io/human-phenotype-ontology/annotations/genes_to_phenotype/ . Fixed top-100 deficit genes per organ in results/strict_organ_deficits.csv. In each list, count unique gene symbols annotated directly to HP:0001939 ("Abnormality of metabolism/homeostasis") by at least one gene-phenotype row. Compare liver versus brain on all 100 top genes with one-sided Fisher exact; H1 liver has higher share, p < 0.05. Ancestor propagation is NOT used and absence of a row is not absence of a clinical phenotype. Record version, mapping rates, fractions for all five organs, and all exact matching symbols. If fewer than 50 of 100 genes in either liver or brain have any HPO annotation, mark primary UNINTERPRETABLE rather than treat missing coverage as a negative. Interpret this as clinical annotation coverage and term association only, not proof of causality or diagnosis. The hypothesis was chosen after examining prior metabolic deficits, so no discovery claim.
