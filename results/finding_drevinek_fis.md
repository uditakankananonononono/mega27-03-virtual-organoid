# Independent multi-patient well-level FIS ranking: pass with important limits

Pre-registered before download at commits `c953790` and `edd828b` (`results/preregistration_drevinek_fis.md`). Source: Drevinek et al., *Journal of Cystic Fibrosis* (2021), DOI 10.1016/j.jcf.2021.07.006, public data and analysis code https://zenodo.org/records/4771466 . Source ZIP SHA-256 `366e4da2e931e96051ad69ac3862a83cd44dff275c97b77e607de6c9622285ae`. Code and full auditable output: `scripts/analyze_drevinek_fis.py`, `results/drevinek_fis.json`.

The source provides **well-aggregate area**, not areas or tracks of individual organoids. It has 15,113 rows, 20 anonymized CF patient IDs, 54 patient-date plates, seven times (0-60 min), and eight forskolin concentrations. Of 432 patient-plate-dose blocks, 431 met the pre-registered >=2 wells per treatment arm and >=3 matched times rule; one block (21-CF, 2020-09-18, 0.02048 µM) had only one TEZ/IVA well. There were no invalid rows or conflicting duplicates. The source plate map assigns FskOnly to reference origin `ref114` and the treatment arms to patient cells: **FskOnly must not be interpreted as a patient-matched vehicle**. This correction was committed after examining the source schema and before outcome analysis.

Primary outcome: equal-well normalized 0-60 minute area AUC, ELX/TEZ/IVA minus TEZ/IVA at matched patient, plate and dose, median across blocks within each patient, then 20 patients given equal weight. **20/20 patient median contrasts positive**, median +0.24884 dimensionless normalized-area AUC (the baseline AUC is 1, so this corresponds to +24.9 percentage points of baseline area averaged across 60 min). Exact one-sided sign p=9.54e-7; the pre-registered H1 gate passed. Among the 431 blocks, 415 contrasts are positive, 16 negative. All 20 patients stay positive when median rather than mean well summary is used; all 20 stay positive under raw-area AUC at the patient median level, yet **47/431 block rankings reverse** under raw-area AUC, showing that normalization matters. One-patient-out: all 19/19 remaining positive in every omission, maximum exact p=1.91e-6. Seventeen patients with multiple plates are 17/17 positive; three one-plate patients are also positive but weaker evidence. Full per-patient and per-block values, the 16 negatives and missingness are preserved in the JSON.

This is an independent biological cohort for the **published triple-over-double in vitro treatment ranking**, not a new discovery: it reanalyzes publicly released data accompanying the original study. It is not a direct independent test of our *small-individual-organoid attenuation* candidate, because no individual baseline/endpoint measurements or raw images are available in this release. The treatment-arm ordering, however strong in these in vitro data, cannot prove benefit for a particular person or support diagnosis, prescribing, or a world-best tool claim. The OrgaSegment held-out segmentation benchmark is still below its published comparison. Do not combine this cohort with the one-donor demo as independent object-level replication.
