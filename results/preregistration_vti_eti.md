# Pre-registration: VTI versus ETI patient-level well-area FIS ranking

Source inspected **metadata only**, no CSV downloaded: Borek-Dohalska, Novotna, Modrák et al., "Effect of vanzacaftor on cystic fibrosis airway epithelial cells compared to elexacaftor", public Zenodo https://zenodo.org/records/14865872 (latest version 15754800, one concept/dataset). `organoids_data.csv` described with anonymized patient ID, date/filename plate, well, 0+ minute times, aggregate area, technical replicate, forskolin concentration, treatment `ETI`, `VTI`, `VTI_2`, `VTI_02`, `VTI_002`, `FskOnly`. Source authors include Drevinek (prior dataset), so **patient overlap is an open possibility**; IDs and clinical provenance will be checked before calling it independent replication. Unpublished raw images and well-level area cannot directly test individual-organoid size attenuation; the separate nasal cell electrophysiology CSV is not part of this test. No therapeutic or diagnostic inference.

Primary matched within patient-date-filename-dose contrast: default `VTI` versus `ETI` only. For each well with finite positive area, unique patient/plate/well/treatment/dose/replicate and time, calculate its baseline-normalized area AUC over 0 to the last time common to all eligible wells in both arms; require baseline and >=3 common time points and >=2 wells/arm/block, excluding conflicting duplicate well/time rows. If times vary by well, calculate only the intersection across *all* eligible wells in that block; no extrapolation, zero-length horizons or outcome-based exclusions. Average normalized AUC equally over technical wells per arm, then VTI minus ETI. Median over plate-dose blocks within each patient (equal patient weight). H1: >=6 eligible patients, >=75% positive patient effects, one-sided exact sign p<.05 among nonzero effects. If criteria fail, report negative/uninterpretable, not fit a replacement cutoff. Report all patient/plate/dose/well counts and exclusions, including all negative strata and source-shared IDs. FskOnly might be a reference line rather than patient-matched; use only for a descriptive source-provenance note, no primary subtraction. If VTI dose mapping is unclear in the released source, stop primary test as uninterpretable pending source code audit, not infer dosage from treatment name.

Prespecified stress tests: (1) equal-well median instead of mean within each block; (2) unnormalized raw area AUC, report block ranking reversals; (3) leave-one-patient-out sign/effect and coverage; (4) per-dose descriptive rates, not treating doses as independent patients; (5) whether lower-dose VTI variants show a monotone ordering by their metadata-reported VTI concentration in matched blocks, exploratory and not pooled into H1. Any patient-ID overlap or nonoverlap with Zenodo 4771466 will be disclosed as *identifier overlap*, not proof of same or different persons across independently anonymized publications. This is an in-vitro re-analysis of published public data, not a new biological discovery.
