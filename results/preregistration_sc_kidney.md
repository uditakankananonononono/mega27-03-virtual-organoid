# Pre-registration: single-cell stress test of the kidney maturation explanation

Registered before download of GSE108291. Source project HCA https://explore.data.humancellatlas.org/projects/7b947aa2-43a7-4082-afff-222a3e3a4635 ; accession GEO GSE108291. Fetch the original human kidney organoid 10x Matrix Market triplet (`org` and `org4` if size permits) from the deposited GEO supplementary files, not the embryonic mouse kidney (`kid`). Do not count the two organoid runs as distinct accession datasets. Analyze with Scanpy and AnnData; preserve provenance and actual mapping quality.

H1: in each organoid run, at least 1% of quality-filtered cells coexpress both proximal-tubule markers ALDOB and SLC17A3 (nonzero UMI), consistent with some mature proximal-tubule-like population. H2: fewer than 20% of cells coexpress both markers, consistent with limited maturity even if a population exists. Thresholds are descriptive operational tests, not clinical cutoffs; record exact counts, the fraction with either marker, and a Wilson 95% CI. Mark UNINTERPRETABLE if genes cannot be uniquely mapped or fewer than 100 QC cells remain. Failing H1 would support absence of this particular two-gene population in these organoids; passing H2 alone does not explain bulk reference mismatch. Different studies and age/protocol limit generalization. Use real raw matrices, no mock counts. No pretense this proves a mechanism.
