# External FIS demo sensitivity: well-level signal and material attrition

Pre-registered at bf80ddc before inspecting the well/track sensitivity results. Source is the same ONE public FIS_image_analysis demo plate, with four wells per forskolin dose (two forskolin-only, two VX-809/VX-770), one donor/genotype. Fixed earlier area quartile thresholds and tracked-object endpoint pairing.

R1, equal-weighting each well rather than pooling its tracks: all eight dose strata pass >=5 paired tracks in both small/large bins for all four wells. Six of eight well-level dose effects are positive, median +0.129 log units. Conditional bootstrap of wells within dose/condition (2,000 seed-27 draws) gives median dose-effect interval [+0.091, +0.161]; pre-registered R1 SUPPORTED. This bootstrap has only two wells per treatment at each dose and does *not* sample donors or laboratories. Doses share one donor/plate, so the interval is not an uncertainty interval for patient-general response.

R2 exposes a substantial selection concern. Baseline uniquely labeled small tracks are retained at t6 in 239/239 forskolin-only and 251/251 combination; large tracks in 260/284 (91.5%) forskolin-only and 230/276 (83.3%) combination. Small-minus-large retention differs by 8.5 percentage points in control and 16.7 points in combination; pre-registered >10-point material-attrition flag is TRUE in combination. Cross-condition difference in the small-minus-large gap is 8.2 points, below its separate 10-point threshold. There are 130 baseline area rows with no track label and 205 endpoint duplicate track/time rows; these counts need annotation-aware review before a strong claim. Size-dependent attrition can shift the observed effect and has not been corrected here.

Result: another conditional one-plate robustness signal, not patient-level replication or an established discovery. The original donor x plate x dose test fails p=.073. Keep both positives and negatives visible, and do not diagnose or improve theratyping based on this.
