# Pre-registration: one-plate FIS demo robustness and track attrition

The first independent-assay analysis of FIS_image_analysis demoplate_01 (commit 4897ce9) found a positive size interaction at seven of eight forskolin doses, but there are only two wells per condition/dose, and track retention could differ by starting size. Before running the following sensitivity tests, lock the prior global A0 size thresholds from `results/external_fis_demo.json` (<=2042.626642 and >=6700.811789 square microns), the 32 downloaded CellProfiler object CSVs and their tracked-ID schema.

R1 (well as measurement unit): within each of the 32 wells, estimate mean log(A60/A0) in small and large bins only if >=5 paired tracks per bin. Calculate large-minus-small within each well, then within each dose calculate mean of the two combo-well contrasts minus mean of the two control-well contrasts, requiring all four wells eligible. Across eligible doses, summarize signs and median, but do not turn correlated doses into independent donors. Also compute a descriptive 95% CI by bootstrapping wells *within each dose/condition*, 2,000 fixed RNG seed 27 replicates; each replicate uses all eight dose effects (or all eligible doses), takes their median; this is well sampling uncertainty conditional on one plate/donor, not a population CI. R1 is supported if >=4 eligible dose strata, positive median, and bootstrap CI lower >0. Otherwise fail, or uninterpretable if fewer strata.

R2 (tracking attrition): at time 0, among positive finite areas and unique positive track IDs per well, count the proportion of small and large baseline objects with a positive finite area at t6, in each condition. Pool eight doses but report per-condition and per-size denominators. Flag material differential attrition if the absolute difference in retention between small and large exceeds 0.10 in either condition, or if cross-condition difference in small-minus-large retention exceeds 0.10. No claim of bias elimination if it does not exceed this threshold. Duplicate track/time rows excluded. Record missing labels and attrition separately. The analysis remains an exploratory assay replication with one genotype, not a donor-level discovery or clinical prediction.
