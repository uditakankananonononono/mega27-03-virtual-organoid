# Internal experiment replication of small-organoid attenuation

The original size thresholds (<722 px, >=1400 px) were locked in `results/preregistration_withincohort_replication.md` and pushed at 8d80877 before this split test. Same primary OrgaSegment DIS accession (Zenodo 10610438), 17 donors, positive forskolin, Trikafta and DMSO. Each donor's first-date experiments were partitioned from later-date experiments. A donor qualified only with at least five organoids in each drug/control by small/large cell. This is *internal* replication across experimental dates, **not an independent dataset or fresh donor cohort**; the cutoff was previously chosen using this accession.

The preregistered later-date test retains 12 donors: all 12 have positive donor-matched large-minus-small drug effect; median attenuation 0.199 log-swelling units; exact one-sided sign p=0.000244. Thus H1 passes. In the earliest-date portion, eight donors qualified, six positive, median 0.264, p=0.145. The date split does not provide a clean training/validation design because thresholds came from the entire accession; it does show that the observed effect recurs on later plate dates with distinct images. Patient-level independence is limited to 12 donor estimates and no independent clinical readout. Per-donor estimates, disqualified cases, and counts are in `results/withincohort_replication.json`.

Named falsifiable candidate: 'small-organoid attenuation' of modulator-induced intestinal-organoid swelling. Prediction for an independent, patient-derived, single-organoid FIS assay: with predeclared size bins and donor-matched DMSO, a majority of donors should show a positive large-minus-small drug effect; measuring lumen formation and CFTR maturation can test mechanism. We do **not** claim an established new discovery, clinical utility, or benchmark superiority. The existing size correction did not improve donor discrimination, and external individual-organoid replication has not been found.
