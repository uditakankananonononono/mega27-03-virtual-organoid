# Matched plate-dose stress test: pre-registered H1 fails

Pre-registration pushed at 87ec71a before evaluating the effect. Same OrgaSegment DIS accession (Zenodo 10610438), original post-hoc size bins (<722, >=1400 px), Trikafta vs DMSO, positive forskolin. Rather than pooling concentrations/plates across each donor, calculate a difference-in-differences only within donor x experiment x forskolin-dose blocks having at least three measured organoids in every treatment-size cell, then take median blocks per donor and test signs across donors.

31 blocks qualify, representing 12 donors; 9/12 donor medians are positive, with one-sided exact sign p=0.072998. The pre-registered H1 is **FAIL**, despite a pooled block median 0.272 log-swelling units. Five donors had no eligible block. Three eligible donor medians are negative (PDIO_01, PDIO_16, PDIO_18), two only slightly so. These are not independent donors from the earlier discovery or date split. This narrower result challenges the apparent 12/12 later-date success: controlling plate and dose directly drops three donor signs, possibly because stringent cells discard observations, but a stronger claim is not warranted. The positive size-response signal remains a candidate, not an established new discovery or clinical/diagnostic improvement. See `results/blocked_size.json` for each donor/block and cell counts.
