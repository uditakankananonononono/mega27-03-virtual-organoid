# Independent single-plate assay corroboration: older double modulator

The pre-registration was committed and pushed as bdc3fcb **before** fetching the full public demo object CSV collection. Source: https://github.com/hmbotelho/FIS_image_analysis ; 32 CellProfiler `objects.csv` files under `demo_dataset/05-images_analysis/demoplate_01--cellprofiler` for the matched forskolin-only (`fsk`) and combined VX-809/VX-770 (`fsk_770_809`) conditions. The README documents one plate of intestinal organoids, one homozygous class-II CFTR genotype, seven 10-minute frames, eight matched forskolin concentrations and two wells per condition/dose. Direct source files and query URLs are listed in `results/external_fis_demo.json`. Paired time 0/6 unique tracks with finite positive area: 1,950 across the 32 wells; 205 candidate duplicate track/time rows removed. Globally pooled A0 quartiles: small <=2042.63 μm² and large >=6700.81 μm². These are not comparable to the pixel thresholds in the original OrgaSegment study.

The primary difference-in-differences of mean log(A60/A0) for combination vs forskolin-only, large vs small, is positive in seven of eight dose strata. Dose effects, in ascending μM: 0.008 -0.0229; 0.02 +0.0095; 0.05 +0.0469; 0.128 +0.0807; 0.32 +0.1690; 0.8 +0.2215; 2 +0.3811; 5 +0.2554. Exact one-sided sign p=0.035156, H1 PASS under the pre-registration, with all eight strata exceeding the cell/well floors. This is an independent *assay source* corroboration of a size-moderated drug effect under another modulator combination, not independent donor replication. Eight doses share one plate and donor; the sign test is over dose strata, whose effects are correlated and not a substitute for donor-level inferential p. The pattern is dose-dependent: near-zero at the smallest concentrations, stronger at higher ones. It is not a new clinical finding, not Trikafta validation, and cannot establish that the effect would hold across patients or laboratories.

Source pipeline and CellProfiler tracking may introduce size-dependent attrition. The original same-accession plate/dose-matched donor test failed (9/12 positive, p=0.073); preserve both. A stricter internal validation in this demo needs well-as-unit uncertainty and a second independent donor cohort; this external one-plate result is promising but not an established discovery. Do not relabel a public demo as 32 datasets, 8 cohorts or 8 independent patients.
