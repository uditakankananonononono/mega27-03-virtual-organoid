# Pre-registration: orthogonal protein-evidence check for top liver deficits

Registered before downloading Human Protein Atlas protein mass-spectrometry tissue intensities. Source page https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/download (HPA v25.1), specifically the protein intensity (MS) 20-tissue table linked there. Fixed top-100 deficit genes: results/strict_organ_deficits.csv. H1: a greater share of *liver* top-100 deficits than *brain* top-100 deficits have detectable protein in the adult liver MS reference, one-sided Fisher p<0.05. Detection threshold is strictly numeric protein intensity >0, not an inferred IHC label. H2: the liver top-100 deficits have a higher median liver-vs-brain log2 protein-intensity contrast than brain top-100 deficits (one-sided Mann-Whitney p<0.05), among genes with both tissues measured. Report missingness by list and labels; mark H1/H2 UNINTERPRETABLE if <50/100 genes mapped or liver/brain tissues absent. This is tissue-reference consistency, not independent organoid measurement or evidence the organoids lack that protein. HPA may share samples/data with GTEx for RNA, hence select MS protein rather than RNA; check metadata.
