# Fiji/ImageJ reanalysis of the SAME intestinal-organoid demo plate

Pre-registered at commit 4aaf86f before fetching 32 Fiji tracked-object CSVs from https://github.com/hmbotelho/FIS_image_analysis . These are **the same images, plate and one donor/genotype** as the CellProfiler result, not a second assay dataset or donor. Fiji's own pooled starting-area quartiles are <=2117.357 and >=8114.459 μm²; 1,938 uniquely paired time-0/time-6 tracks from the selected 32 wells. The primary matched-dose combo (VX-809/VX-770) versus vehicle size interaction is positive at 7/8 doses (all except 0.008 μM), median +0.225 log units, so the preregistered H1 PASSES. Dose effects ascending: -0.003, +0.018, +0.012, +0.145, +0.305, +0.603, +0.553, +0.529. Equal-well descriptive effects positive at all eight doses, median +0.226. No independent-patient p-value is computed.

Tracking behavior differs by pipeline. Fiji has 0 baseline rows missing a tracking ID and 14 duplicate endpoint track/time rows; unique-labeled small retention is 237/255 (92.9%) vehicle and 227/256 (88.7%) combo; large is 312/317 (98.4%) vehicle and 300/320 (93.8%) combo. In contrast, CellProfiler showed poorer large-track retention in the combo arm (83.3%). This difference suggests a pipeline-dependent selection effect and reinforces caution about effect magnitude, even though the interaction sign is similar across the two pipelines. Their tracks, segmentation areas and cutoffs are not interchangeable, and the two passes are not two independent biological replications. Source paths, per-dose cell/well counts and retention: `results/external_fis_fiji.json`. The original distinct multi-donor OrgaSegment stricter plate/dose stress remains negative (9/12 donors, p=.073). No validated patient-general discovery, theratyping benefit or diagnosis follows.
