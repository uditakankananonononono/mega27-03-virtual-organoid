# Berical et al. 2022 supplemental-source suitability

Preregistered inspection plan: `results/preregistration_berical_source_audit.md` (commit 096acf5, before download). Primary paper: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-31854-8 . Source Data: https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-022-31854-8/MediaObjects/41467_2022_31854_MOESM7_ESM.xlsx (316,844 bytes). Downloaded to temporary inspection only, not a primary-used accession.

Workbook sheets: Fig 1C, 1D, 2E, 2F, 3B, 3D, 4B, 4F, 5A, 5B, 5F, 5H. The displayed 1000-row worksheet dimensions in several sheets largely reflect formatting, not 1000 measured objects. Fig 2E has three non-CF donors' numbers of spheres analyzed per experiment. Fig 2F has three donors' vehicle/forskolin response values summarized per experiment, not raw object trajectories. Fig 3B shows baseline size values grouped as non-CF, Phe508del, G551D, and premature-stop variants, without object, treatment, or time IDs. Fig 3D has three Phe508del and three G551D patient lines' forskolin and modulator condition CSA-percent values, typically three to six numeric replicate values per arm per line; they do not carry links to the Fig 3B sizes. The remaining sheets are expression, electrophysiology and other figure aggregates.

Verdict: the *paper* tracked single airway spheroids (Fig 2) and demonstrated multi-line modulator FIS (Fig 3), so it is relevant prior art against broad single-object novelty claims. The released workbook does **not** provide multi-donor matched-treatment object-level baseline/end pairs; computing a size-moderation result from these separate aggregate columns would be pseudopairing. Different tissue, differentiation platform and 20-24-hour assay also preclude treating it as direct external intestinal replication. The authors' data statement allows reasonable requests for underlying data; absence in this workbook is not proof of nonexistence. No new discovery or gate increment.
