# Exact published AP scorer does not produce a benchmark win

Locked plan: `results/preregistration_benchmark_reproduction.md`, committed at `855f6be` before running this check. Authors' release `Living-Technologies/OrgaSegment` tag v1.0.1, commit `aeaec17c42fefde61a8b2215a616d419e8126e9f`, source https://github.com/Living-Technologies/OrgaSegment/tree/v1.0.1 . Our fixed 512-px model, three-view TTA and previously selected postprocessing (.5 foreground, .5 seed, .5 boundary, minimum 40 scaled by image area) were applied unchanged to the 12 original eval images, then the **same predictions** were passed to the original `lib/metrics.py` Cellpose AP and our scorer (`scripts/audit_benchmark_metric.py`). All predicted IDs were relabeled contiguous without changing pixels because the original scorer defines predicted count as the maximum ID.

The original scorer gives mean AP@0.5 **0.73236**; our prior score is **0.73295**. Eleven images agree to numerical rounding. The twelfth has an absent GT label ID 17 among 1..87: the released scorer treats `max(GT)=87` as 87 objects even though only 86 unique positive masks exist, producing 0.73077 instead of 0.73786 on that image. This is an indexing discrepancy, not a model improvement or fix to apply post hoc. The original study published **0.76 +/- 0.12** on 12 images (https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-05966-4). We remain below it under either implementation. Released Mask R-CNN weights exist, but its v1.0.1 evaluation script requires GPU and TensorFlow/Mask-RCNN dependencies unavailable in this workspace; no paired published-model predictions were generated. These small numerical differences cannot rescue benchmark superiority or establish statistically matched performance. Full per-image AP and TP/FP/FN are in `results/benchmark_metric_audit.json`.
