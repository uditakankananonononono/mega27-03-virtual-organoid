# Pre-registration: independent WikiPathways pathway annotation of deficit genes

Fixed top-100 gene symbols for each of five organs: results/strict_organ_deficits.csv. Register before fetching the 2026-09-10 Homo sapiens WikiPathways GMT from https://data.wikipathways.org/20260910/gmt/ (per https://www.wikipathways.org/download.html). The GMT uses Entrez Gene IDs, to be mapped to official human symbols using the NCBI Homo_sapiens.gene_info.gz gene table https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/DATA/GENE_INFO/Mammalia/Homo_sapiens.gene_info.gz ; ambiguous symbols excluded. Gene mapping is annotation, not another primary dataset.

Primary H1: the *union* of pathways whose case-insensitive name contains any of the fixed substrings `bile acid`, `fatty acid`, `drug metabolism`, `xenobiotic metabolism` has a higher overlap among liver top-100 deficits than brain top-100 deficits (one-sided Fisher p < 0.05). A name match must come from the actual GMT name; do not choose extra favorable pathways after inspection. Report matching names and mapped pathway genes, counts by organ, mapping coverage; H1 uninterpretable if fewer than two matching pathways or fewer than 80 mapped deficit symbols per group. This tests an independent resource's annotation consistency, not an independent cohort or proof of a causal mechanism. Overlapping pathways and post hoc organ contrast limit interpretation. Keep negative or uninformative outcomes.
