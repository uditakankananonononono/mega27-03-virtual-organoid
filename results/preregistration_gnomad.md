# Pre-registration: population constraint of organoid deficits

Registered before fetching the gnomAD constraint file. Source: official gnomAD v2.1.1 gene-level pLoF metrics, https://storage.googleapis.com/gcp-public-data--gnomad/release/2.1.1/constraint/gnomad.v2.1.1.lof_metrics.by_gene.txt.bgz ; metric: oe_lof_upper (LOEUF), a higher value means less LoF constraint. gnomAD explains interpretation at https://gnomad.broadinstitute.org/help/constraint . v2 scores cannot be treated as equivalent to v4 thresholds.

The held-fixed organoid deficit lists are results/strict_organ_deficits.csv, top 100 per organ. Gene symbols will be matched to the `gene` column of the gnomAD gene-level file (one gene per row); ambiguous duplicate symbols excluded. Compare liver and brain deficits on the gnomAD-mapped genes by one-sided Mann-Whitney U, H1: liver median LOEUF > brain median LOEUF, p < 0.05. Rationale: missing liver metabolic enzymes may be less haploinsufficient than missing cortical developmental genes. Report mapped n, medians, U, p; also report all five organs and pairwise kidney-vs-brain as descriptive, not confirmatory. If source inaccessible or mapping <30 for either group, mark test uninterpretable, not pass. Do not infer causal fidelity from constraint, and note correlated gene sets and the post hoc choice of organs.
