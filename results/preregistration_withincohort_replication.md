# Internal replication by experiment date: size attenuation

This is NOT an independent dataset: OrgaSegment DIS accession is the original discovery source. Split 17 donors chronologically by their experiment date (experiment text's first YYYYMMDD; HitCF dates taken embedded YYYYMMDD), earliest donor-experiment as discovery-like portion, all later experiments as internal replication. Fixed, previously identified size cutoffs A0 < 722 px and A0 >=1400 px, forskolin >0, conditions Trikafta (VX445_VX661_VX770) versus DMSO. For each donor and portion, estimate difference-in-differences of mean log(swelling): (drug-large minus control-large) minus (drug-small minus control-small). Retain donors with >=5 measured organoids in each of the four drug/control-size cells. Primary H1: on later experiments only, median donor-level attenuation >0 via exact one-sided binomial sign test p<0.05; require >=8 donors. Report first-experiment and later-experiment estimates separately; no further threshold selection. The cutoff is post hoc with respect to the entire accession, and subjects/plate work overlap; a positive internal replication supports robustness but not independent discovery or clinical utility. Keep negative outcomes.
