# Pre-registration: Rhea curated reaction mapping of fixed organoid deficits

Before downloading the Rhea release mapping at https://ftp.expasy.org/databases/rhea/tsv/rhea2uniprot_sprot.tsv, lock the five top-100 lists in results/strict_organ_deficits.csv. Map their symbols to UniProt accession IDs using the already-downloaded EBI GOA human GAF, retaining taxon 9606 and DB=UniProtKB (symbol can have multiple IDs). A deficit symbol has a curated Rhea reaction if any mapped accession occurs in the Rhea Swiss-Prot mapping file; deduplicate Rhea IDs and count each symbol once. H1: liver top-deficit symbols have more Rhea-mapped reaction annotations than brain cortex top-deficit symbols, one-sided Fisher p<0.05. Require GOA UniProt accessions for >=80 genes in each list and >=20 Rhea-positive genes across the union of all five lists, otherwise uninterpretable. Report five organ counts, unmapped symbols and Rhea example identifiers. Rhea annotations may be closely linked to UniProt/GO/KEGG and are not independent experimental organoid data. Selection of organ contrast follows earlier exploration; PASS is pathway-label corroboration only, not mechanism or diagnostic discovery.
