# Vanzacaftor-versus-elexacaftor well-level FIS: negative pre-registered test

Source: https://zenodo.org/records/15754800 (Borek-Dohalska/Novotna/Modrák et al., concept link https://zenodo.org/records/14865872). Pre-registration `results/preregistration_vti_eti.md`, frozen and pushed at commit `08c5e22` before downloading the data. CSV SHA-256 `46dbbf719d9c4fe4d195508fd81446f4e6e0e03e5f79bc45735851b0fae1ffc4`. Script `scripts/analyze_vti_eti.py`, complete positive and negative block output `results/vti_eti.json`.

**Primary H1 failed.** The public area time series has 10,864 rows, 12 anonymized patient IDs, 33 patient-date plates, seven 0-60 minute points and eight forskolin doses. Three patients have dose-optimization arms but no default `VTI`; a fourth plate has ETI but no matched default VTI. The registered *default VTI vs ETI* comparison is therefore eligible for 9 patients and 208 of 264 patient-plate-dose blocks. The other 56 blocks lack both arms and were excluded; no patient dose-optimization rows were swapped into the primary test. There were no invalid area rows or conflicting duplicates. Within each matched block, the equal-well mean baseline-normalized area AUC for VTI minus ETI was computed; patient medians are positive in **6/9**, median +0.01814 normalized-area AUC, exact one-sided sign p=0.25390625. The registered requirements (>=6 patients, >=75% positive and p<.05) do not pass. Across blocks, 112/208 are positive and 96/208 nonpositive. Patient IDs 31, 40 and 68 are negative; see JSON for their actual effects and every plate/dose result.

Stress tests: median-of-wells also 6/9 positive; raw-area AUC only 3/9 positive and reverses 92/208 individual block rankings relative to normalized AUC. Every leave-one-patient-out result remains below the registered positive-fraction/p threshold; the largest sign p is 0.3633. Dose-optimization subset (three different patient IDs, 48 complete plate-dose blocks) is **descriptive only**: labels `VTI_002`, `VTI_02`, `VTI_2` correspond to vanzacaftor 0.02, 0.2 and 2 µM according to the source's `organoids.qmd`. Strict ascending normalized AUC across all three concentrations occurs in 12/48 blocks. Relative to ETI the block median effects are -0.0413, +0.0187 and +0.00434, not monotone medians. This is not an inferential dose-response test.

Patient IDs `58` and `68` overlap textually with Zenodo 4771466 after stripping the earlier `-CF` suffix. Because these releases use anonymized identifiers, this is an overlap warning, not proof of same people or independent people. Only `68` contributes to this default-VTI primary analysis and its patient effect is negative; `58` is in dose optimization only. The source's raw plate images are unpublished and its area is a **well sum**, so this cannot test single-organoid size attenuation. A negative VTI-versus-ETI ranking in this design is not evidence of equivalence or inferiority in patients and should not inform treatment decisions. The earlier 20/20 triple-versus-double result is a different contrast and remains as reported; do not merge these tests into a single pass claim.
