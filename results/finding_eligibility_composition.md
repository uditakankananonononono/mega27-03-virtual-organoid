# Why min-10's six positive donors do not rescue the primary negative

This donor-composition audit was frozen at commit 2364e96 before reading the min-10 IDs, using only `results/block_eligibility_sensitivity.json`. At min 3, 12 donors qualify, nine positive and three nonpositive (`PDIO_01`, `PDIO_16`, `PDIO_18`). At min 5 the same 12 qualify; `PDIO_15` changes from positive to negative because its block median changes when a marginal cell is excluded. At min 10, six donors qualify (`PDIO_03`, `PDIO_05`, `PDIO_06`, `PDIO_09`, `PDIO_13`, `PDIO_14`), all of whom were already positive at min 3. **All three min-3 negative donors are excluded**, along with three previously positive donors (`PDIO_11`, `PDIO_12`, `PDIO_15`). At min 20, only `PDIO_03`, `PDIO_05`, `PDIO_06` qualify. Five of the original 17 OrgaSegment donors did not meet even min 3.

Thus the nominal 6/6 positive at min 10 is a smaller selected donor population, below the preregistered >=8 eligible-donor floor. This does not prove why a donor lacked eligible size/treatment cells, nor justify treating excluded donors as negative outcomes. It does show precisely why reporting 6/6 without its denominator would mislead. No new biological gate or p-value is claimed.
